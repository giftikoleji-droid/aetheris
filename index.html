Yield Engine

What powers the investment tiers

Capital is allocated through a Hybrid CeDeFi Yield Framework — pure-crypto mechanisms across different market conditions.

Strategy A

Funding rate arbitrage

Delta-neutral style positioning aims to collect funding paid by leveraged traders, reducing pure directional bets as the main driver.

Strategy B

DeFi lending & vaults

Stablecoin capital in established lending-style markets where borrowers pay interest that can be compounded programmatically.

Strategy C

Liquid staking & restaking

Participation in proof-of-stake network economics via liquid staking pathways and optional restaking layers where used.

Strategy D

Liquidity & efficiency

Automated liquidity and cross-venue efficiency seek fees and short-lived spreads — typically more active at higher tiers.
